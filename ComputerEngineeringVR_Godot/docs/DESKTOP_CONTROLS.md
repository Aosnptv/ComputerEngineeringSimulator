# Desktop controls

- WASD: move
- Mouse: look
- E or left mouse: activate targeted 3D equipment
- Esc: capture/release mouse; the pointer starts released for accessible panel controls
- H: request hint
- R: recenter/reset context
- Q/C: turn alternatives

The center reticle displays the target label and pickup range is four metres. Panel controls expose the identical mission commands for accessibility and testability; they do not bypass validators.
